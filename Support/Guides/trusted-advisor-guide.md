## 🎯 Activity Objective

**Explore AWS Support Center and Trusted Advisor** to understand support options and automated best practice recommendations.

**⏱️ Estimated Time:** 10-15 minutes  
**💰 Cost:** Free  
**🔧 Difficulty:** Beginner

---

## 🚀 Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Access Support Center 🆘

1. Click the **"?"** icon in top-right menu
2. Select **"Support Center"**
3. View your current support plan (AWS Basic Support)

### Step 2: Navigate to Trusted Advisor 🔍

1. In console search bar, type **"Trusted Advisor"**
2. Click on **"Trusted Advisor"** service
3. View the dashboard

### Step 3: Review Check Categories 📊

**Trusted Advisor checks 5 categories:**
1. **Cost Optimization** 💰
2. **Performance** ⚡
3. **Security** 🔒
4. **Fault Tolerance** 🛡️
5. **Service Limits** 📏

### Step 4: Examine Security Checks 🔒

1. Click **"Security"** category
2. Look for **"MFA on Root Account"** check
3. Should show **Green checkmark** ✅ (completed in Activity 1)

### Step 5: Review Service Limits 📏

1. Click **"Service Limits"** category
2. Review current usage vs. limits
3. Note any approaching limits
